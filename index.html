Independent technical exploration

Reliable infrastructure for agent systems.

Agents can execute tasks. The systems problem is supervising that execution: preserving state, defining authority, recovering from failure, and retaining evidence about what happened.

Agent Supervisor ↗ Read the case study ↗

The observation

Capability is moving faster than operational trust.

As models become capable enough to perform real work, the surrounding infrastructure becomes more important: lifecycle management, tool boundaries, interruption, durable history, and honest recovery when an outcome cannot be confirmed.
